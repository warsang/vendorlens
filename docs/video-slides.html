the problem

“Is this counterparty a real business, or a front?”

A URL checker tells you whether a page is malicious. It cannot tell you whether the company behind it exists.

VendorLens answers with evidence, not vibes

Eleven OSINT tools, a planner that orders them, an observer that replans on surprises, weighted risk signals across three layers, and a brief where every claim cites the tool and source behind it and carries a confidence tag.

the agent · failure, retry, recovery

When a source fails, the run continues

# examples/injected-failure-acme.example/trace.jsonl (current code)
tool_retry ct_logs attempt 1: simulated 503 (fault injection)
tool_retry ct_logs attempt 2: simulated 503 (fault injection)
tool_recovered ct_logs attempts: 3 (transient only, jitter + cap)

Classified retries

Only 429 / 5xx / timeout retry. A 404 or NXDOMAIN is recorded once, with no wasted sleeps.

Reproducible

--inject-failure ct_logs:2 fails the first two attempts so the recovery is visible on camera.

The archived runs/amazon.com/…/trace.jsonl shows the earlier behaviour: fixed sleeps retried even a deterministic "feed too large" error.
