the problem

“Is this counterparty a real business, or a front?”

A URL checker tells you whether a page is malicious. It cannot tell you whether the company behind it exists. Before money or data goes to a new vendor, somebody has to check registration, DNS, certificates, scans, reviews and company records.

VendorLens answers with evidence, not vibes

Nine OSINT tools, a planner that orders them, an observer that replans on surprises, weighted risk signals, and a brief where every claim cites the tool and source behind it.

the agent · failure, retry, recovery

When a source fails, the run continues

# runs/amazon.com/20261011T203036Z/trace.jsonl (real live run)
tool_retry phishtank_check attempt 1: PhishTank HTTP 503
tool_retry phishtank_check attempt 2: PhishTank HTTP 503 (backoff 2s → 4s)
tool_retry phishtank_check attempt 3: PhishTank HTTP 503 (backoff 8s)
gave up after 3 — recorded as a failed step, run continues
run_end score 12 band LOW — brief still written, every claim still cited

Per-step retry with exponential backoff (vendorlens/agent.py:62), failures never fatal (agent.py:78), and the whole sequence lives in the trace.
